Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Overview

Jira Sprint Dashboard is an internal dashboard that displays Jira sprint issues. It uses Atlassian OAuth 2.0 (3LO) to read data from Jira on behalf of the signed-in user.

Data We Access

  • Jira issue metadata such as key, summary, status, priority, assignee, and changelog.
  • Board and sprint information required to identify the active sprint.

Data We Do Not Store

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept only in browser memory for the duration of the session.
  • We do not persist tokens, personal data, or Jira content on our servers.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising cookies.

How Data Is Used

The accessed Jira data is used solely to render the dashboard in the user's browser. No data is shared with third parties.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the dashboard administrator within your organization.
